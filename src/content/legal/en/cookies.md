## What cookies are

Small files your browser stores when you visit a website.

## Cookies we use

| Cookie         | Provider         | Purpose                    | Duration |
| -------------- | ---------------- | -------------------------- | -------- |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics | Anonymous usage statistics | 2 years  |

Analytics cookies are only enabled if you accept them in the cookie notice.

## Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time from the "Cookie settings" link in the footer or by deleting cookies in your browser.
