## Data controller

The controller is the website owner identified in the legal notice.

## Purpose

We process the data you provide through the form or by email to answer your enquiries and, where applicable, prepare a quote.

## Legal basis

The legal basis is your consent and the performance of pre-contractual measures.

## Retention

We keep the data for as long as needed to handle your request and for the applicable legal periods.

## Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to the contact email. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency.
